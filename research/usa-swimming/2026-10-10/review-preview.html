USA Swimming — national organization and directory structure

National governing body (documented in a national structure reference)

https://www.usaswimming.org/

USA Swimming groups LSCs into four administrative zones. Article 606 permits regional competition arrangements between LSCs; it does not establish a mandatory zone → region → LSC hierarchy. Region entries in this batch describe competition groupings, not an additional governing tier.

Geographic coverage: United States; LSC jurisdiction follows Article 603 rather than state borders alone.

Collection coverage: Complete inventory of the four zone names; detailed first-zone collection covers Eastern Zone. Nationwide LSC and region collection remains partial.
