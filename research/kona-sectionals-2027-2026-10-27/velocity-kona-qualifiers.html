Entry rules

The 800m/1000y and 1500m/1650y freestyle cuts qualify reciprocally for both distance events. Brian and Caroline each qualify for the 1000 and 1650 under that provision. Times are compared in the course actually swum, without conversions. Flying-start relay splits are excluded. The meet permits up to six individual events, three per day, and up to two bonus events for a swimmer with an individual qualifying event.

These are source-verified qualifying-time matches. This audit updates the report; it does not publish new race records to the live database. Current USA Swimming registration, team membership, proof of times and host acceptance remain entry checks.

Sources: Kona Aquatics host page, sanctioned meet announcement, Velocity’s Swimcloud roster, and each swimmer’s all-course time table and event history. Every qualifying time links to its source result. Snapshot: October 27, 2026.
